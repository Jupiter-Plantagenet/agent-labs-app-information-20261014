Privacy policy: Agent Labs Archive

This personal-use connector performs owner-authorized backup, integrity checking, listing, download, and restoration of selected artifacts through Google Drive.

Data and permissions

These operations process selected file contents and associated names, folder and file identifiers, sizes, checksums, and transfer records. The configured archive folder limits the connector's intended operations; it does not itself narrow an account-wide OAuth permission granted by Google. The owner should review the actual permissions on the Google consent screen.

Processing and storage

Selected files are processed on the owner's local platform and stored in the owner's Google Drive. OAuth credentials are stored in private local credential storage, separately from the intended artifact archive allowlists. This informational website collects no credentials and contains no analytics script, contact form, or advertising. Its hosting provider may process ordinary request metadata.

Purpose and disclosure

The archive connector is not an advertising, data-sale, or model-training service. It does not itself run cloud model inference. Separate research features and providers are subject to the owner's separately approved data policies; this page does not imply that approved cloud inference providers cannot process research context deliberately supplied to them. Account credentials and unrelated personal data are not approved research inputs. Archived files are not intentionally made public by the connector. Google and any separately approved service process supplied data under their own applicable terms.

Retention and control

The owner controls retention of local and Drive copies. Verified preservation and the owner's operating policy are required before removing useful local artifacts. Revoking the application's access through Google Account settings stops its authorized future access, but does not itself delete previously stored copies. The owner can remove selected copies using the relevant local and Drive controls. The connector must pause or report failed authorization rather than bypass it.

Questions can be directed to the support contact configured in the application's Google authorization settings. This policy concerns the personal archive connector, not a public multi-user service or an assurance against compromise of the host or provider.

Personal-use app information. No credentials, research artifacts, or protected evaluation material are published here.

Agent Labs Archive

A personal-use Google Drive connector for an owner-operated research platform.

The connector backs up selected local artifacts to the owner's Google Drive, reads back file identities and integrity information, and restores selected archives to local storage. It is not a publicly offered multi-user service.

Google authorization is handled by the native client. This informational website does not collect passwords, authorization codes, or account tokens and does not offer a sign-in form.

The archive component is separate from research agents, model providers, and scientific evaluation. Research tasks require their own owner-approved problem and data policies; this website does not authorize a research campaign.

Support is provided by the owner using the support contact configured for the application's Google authorization settings.

Personal-use app information. No credentials, research artifacts, or protected evaluation material are published here.
